Notice 01 — Open

Community Greening Liaison

We are seeking residents who care about Cobbs Creek Park and want to help shape its future. Community Greening Liaisons connect the planning process with their neighbors by sharing updates, gathering input, and supporting outreach at meetings and events. Their involvement helps ensure the priorities of West and Southwest Philadelphia residents guide the work.

Liaisons bring community perspectives to Steering Committee meetings and planning activities, help establish priorities, and strengthen local partnerships. They also support the transition from planning to implementation.

We welcome nominations from across the planning area, including Overbrook, Carroll Park, Haddington, Cobbs Creek, Kingsessing, Paschall, and Elmwood.

Nominations closeMon, Nov 16, 2026
TermFall 2026 – Fall 2027
Time, compensated10–15 hours / month

Nominate yourself or a neighbor who would bring valuable community knowledge and perspective to this role.

The Steering Committee reviews and scores all applications. Top-ranked applicants may be invited to interview or provide additional information before final selections are made.

Are you between the ages of 16 and 25? The Steering Committee wants to hear your voice too, and encourages you to apply.

Notice 02 — Open

Plan Development Consultant

We are seeking a consultant or planning team to help residents shape the future of Cobbs Creek Park, its trails, and surrounding neighborhoods. The consultant will guide community engagement, assess existing conditions and related projects, and bring technical information together with local knowledge to develop an implementation-ready greening plan.

Working under the Steering Committee’s direction, the consultant will translate community priorities into specific projects, recommended partnerships, timelines, and funding pathways. The work will also establish a framework for continued coordination and long-term stewardship.

We welcome applicants with experience in community-led planning, inclusive engagement, and collaboration across organizations and public agencies. Strong proposals will demonstrate respect for resident expertise and a commitment to keeping community members central to decisions.

Applications dueFri, Nov 27, 2026
Written questions dueTue, Nov 17, 2026
Project durationAbout 12 months

Review the full RFP for the scope of work, required qualifications, proposal instructions, and evaluation criteria.

The Steering Committee evaluates proposals based on community engagement and priorities, technical skills, and the ability to develop an actionable plan. Firms owned by people of color or women, and consultants who reside in the Cobbs Creek area, are strongly encouraged to apply.
